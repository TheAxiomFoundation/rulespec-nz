# Track 33 Specification: Rates rebates and local-government-adjacent assistance

## Objective

Encode rates rebate and related local-government assistance surfaces in RuleSpec NZ as a distinct track, covering rebate eligibility and threshold logic.

## Scope

- Rates rebate eligibility and maximum amount
- Income threshold and contribution calculation
- Local-authority interaction and timing rules
- Companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- GST
- general income tax
- student support

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant rates rebate modules are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and DIA guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
