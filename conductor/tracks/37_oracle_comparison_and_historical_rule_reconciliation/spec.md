# Track 37 Specification: Oracle comparison and historical rule reconciliation

## Objective

Build comparison and historical-rule reconciliation infrastructure for RuleSpec NZ as a small non-legislative track.

## Scope

- Pinned oracle and comparison manifests for completed tracks
- Historical-rule comparison slices for date-effective validation
- A narrow reconciliation harness that stays separate from legal source text

## Out of Scope

- New legislation conversion
- source ingestion
- oracle-authoritative rewrites

## Acceptance Criteria

- Pinned comparison manifests exist for the targeted completed tracks.
- The reconciliation harness is non-authoritative and separate from legal source text.
- The track is small enough to be reviewed independently of legislation conversion work.
