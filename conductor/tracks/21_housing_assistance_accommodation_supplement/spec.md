# Track 21: Housing Assistance and Accommodation Supplement

## Scope

Encode housing assistance surfaces, prioritising Accommodation Supplement, with official source provenance and tests.

## In Scope

- Accommodation Supplement eligibility.
- Accommodation areas and rates.
- Cash asset tests.
- Income cutouts and abatement.
- Boarder/lodger and accommodation cost predicates.
- Social housing eligibility interfaces where required for benefit interactions.

## Out of Scope

- Local authority operational rules not needed for central policy eligibility.
- Unverified secondary summaries.
- Oracle code as legal authority.

## Acceptance Criteria

- RuleSpec modules and tests cover core Accommodation Supplement scenarios.
- Area/rate schedules are tied to official source evidence.
- Housing Restructuring interfaces are represented only where needed for central eligibility.
- Upstream issue `#34` is referenced in the implementation PR.
