# Track 39 Specification: Dynamic simulation and research extensions

## Objective

Record the dynamic and research extension bucket as a narrow track so it does not expand the legislation backlog.

## Scope

- Dynamic microsimulation interfaces
- Policy diffusion and ABM/DES feasibility slices
- Value-of-information and research-oriented outputs

## Out of Scope

- New legislation conversion
- source ingestion
- routine valuation or tax logic

## Acceptance Criteria

- The research bucket is explicitly separated from legislation conversion tracks.
- Subcomponents stay narrow enough to split further if needed.
- The track references existing encoded surfaces but does not duplicate them.
