# Track 38 Specification: Corpus citation pinning and provenance QA

## Objective

Pin corpus citation paths and add provenance QA so future legislation tracks have a smaller source-evidence surface.

## Scope

- Corpus citation path pinning
- Source-manifest provenance QA
- A narrow checklist for evidence hygiene before new legislation tracks are opened

## Out of Scope

- Legislation encoding itself
- oracle comparison logic
- broad repo cleanup

## Acceptance Criteria

- Corpus citation paths are pinned for the targeted source families.
- The provenance QA surface is small and does not absorb legislation work.
- The track produces durable evidence that future tracks can reuse.
