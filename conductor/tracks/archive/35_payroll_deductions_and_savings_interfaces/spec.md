# Track 35 Specification: Payroll deductions and savings interfaces

## Objective

Encode payroll deduction and savings interfaces in RuleSpec NZ as a distinct track, keeping withholding and savings mechanics separate from direct tax rates.

## Scope

- KiwiSaver employee and employer contribution surfaces
- Student loan deduction mechanics
- PAYE periodization and payroll interfaces
- Companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- general benefits
- housing assistance
- family payments

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant payroll deduction and savings modules are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and IRD guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
