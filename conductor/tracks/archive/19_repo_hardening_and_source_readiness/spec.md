# Track 19: Repo Hardening and Source Readiness

## Scope

Track 19 records the repository-level quality gates and source-readiness evidence required before further full-country RuleSpec expansion.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Quality commands are discoverable from `pixi.toml`.
- CI records both reusable RuleSpec validation and roadmap coverage checks.
- The source-readiness manifest references the canonical source map and backlog.
- The PCO corpus locator is available and has no current blockers.
- Agency source inventory statuses are explicitly enumerated for follow-on implementation tracks.
