# Track 36 Specification: GST and indirect-tax interfaces

## Objective

Encode GST and indirect-tax interfaces in RuleSpec NZ as a distinct track, covering the core GST conversion surfaces and related indirect-tax inputs.

## Scope

- GST inclusive and exclusive conversion surfaces
- Low-value import interfaces
- Excise-adjacent indirect-tax hooks for future analysis
- Companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- direct income tax brackets
- benefit rates
- family assistance

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant GST and indirect-tax modules are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and IRD guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
