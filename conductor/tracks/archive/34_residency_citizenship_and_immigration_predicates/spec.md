# Track 34 Specification: Residency, citizenship, and immigration predicates

## Objective

Encode the residency, citizenship, and immigration predicate surfaces used by fiscal programs in RuleSpec NZ as a distinct track.

## Scope

- Citizenship and residence predicates
- Visa class and indefinite-stay predicates
- Program-specific residence duration tests
- Companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- tax rate setting
- benefit rate tables
- social housing formula surfaces

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant residency, citizenship, and immigration predicates are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and agency guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
