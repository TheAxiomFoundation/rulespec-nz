# Track 31 Specification: Childcare, disability, and health-related assistance

## Objective

Encode childcare, disability, and health-related assistance surfaces in RuleSpec NZ as a distinct track, covering assistance eligibility, rate, and interaction logic.

## Scope

- Childcare subsidy and out-of-school care support
- Disability and community-services assistance
- Primary-care copayment and health-adjacent assistance
- Companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- Paid parental leave surfaces
- child support surfaces
- student support surfaces

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant childcare, disability, and health-related assistance modules are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and MSD guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
