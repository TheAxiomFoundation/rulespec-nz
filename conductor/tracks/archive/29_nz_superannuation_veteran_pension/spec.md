# Track 29 Specification: NZ Superannuation and veteran pension surfaces

## Objective

Encode NZ Superannuation and veteran pension surfaces in RuleSpec NZ as a
distinct track, covering age and residence qualification, ordinary rates,
special rates, hospital reductions, and cross-benefit interactions.

## Scope

- age qualification and residence qualification
- ordinary weekly rates
- special rates and hospital rates
- long-term residential care reductions
- veteran pension interactions and cross-benefit exclusions
- companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- Working for Families surfaces
- housing assistance surfaces
- personal income tax gap-fill surfaces

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant NZ Superannuation and veteran pension modules are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and MSD guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
