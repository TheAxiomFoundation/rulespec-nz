# Track 32 Specification: Paid parental leave, child support, and family-related payments

## Objective

Encode paid parental leave and child support surfaces in RuleSpec NZ as a distinct track, keeping the family-payment formula logic separate from unrelated income-support families.

## Scope

- Paid parental leave eligibility, rate, and duration
- Child support formula and liable-parent tests
- Receiving-carer and transfer-of-entitlement rules
- Companion tests for boundary and date-effective cases

## Out of Scope

- Student support surfaces
- housing assistance surfaces
- rates rebates surfaces

## Acceptance Criteria

- The relevant paid parental leave and child support modules are represented in RuleSpec with source-linked tests.
- The track has local source evidence tied to official legislation and IRD guidance.
- Comparison references remain non-authoritative and are recorded separately from legal source text.
- The roadmap reflects the dependency order for the next legislation families.
